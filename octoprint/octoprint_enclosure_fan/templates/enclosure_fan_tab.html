PID regulation

Output is kp·error + ki·integral + kd·derivative, driving the louvre across the lower half of its travel and the fan across the upper half. Apply changes the running gains only; they are lost on reboot. Save also writes them to the board's NVS so they survive a power cycle.

Accepted ranges: kp , ki , kd . Running gains are not saved — they will be lost on reboot.

PID calibration

Runs the board's relay-feedback auto-tune. The bed is heated to 90 °C, then the louvre and fan oscillate around a 35 °C chamber setpoint. The printer must be idle. The result can be applied to RAM, applied and saved to NVS, or discarded.

Calibration is disabled while the printer is busy.

Start PID calibration?

  • The printer must be idle — no print or paused job.
  • The bed will be heated to 90 °C and switched off when the run ends.
  • The louvre and fan run for up to 60 minutes after the chamber reaches 35 °C.
  • The chamber is held near 35 °C; the run aborts if it exceeds 50 °C.
  • Commands from the CYD are ignored for the duration.

Manual control

Unavailable while the CYD is linked. Unavailable while PID calibration is running.

Manual mode bypasses the regulator entirely. Switching back to Automatic while the display is unplugged leaves the board on its conservative 50 °C fallback target, because this endpoint does not claim to know the material.
