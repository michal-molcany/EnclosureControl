PID regulation

Output is kp·error + ki·integral + kd·derivative, driving the louvre across the lower half of its travel and the fan across the upper half. Apply changes the running gains only; they are lost on reboot. Save also writes them to the board's NVS so they survive a power cycle.

Accepted ranges: kp , ki , kd . Running gains are not saved — they will be lost on reboot.

Manual control

Unavailable while the CYD is linked.

Manual mode bypasses the regulator entirely. Switching back to Automatic while the display is unplugged leaves the board on its conservative 50 °C fallback target, because this endpoint does not claim to know the material.
